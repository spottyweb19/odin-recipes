How to make Sunny Side up

Ingredients:

  • Egg
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Oil

Instructions:

  1. Heat Frying pan
  2. Pour Oil on the Frying pan
  3. Once theres barely a bit of smoke, crack the Egg in the pan
  4. Sprinkle desired amount of Salt and Pepper on the Egg
  5. Then wait till Egg white is no longer transluscent
  6. All set and Ready to serve!
