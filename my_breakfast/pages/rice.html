How to make Rice

Ingredients:

  • Rice
  • Water

Instructions:

  1. Wash Rice thoroughly till it's clear
  2. Transfer the Rice in the rice cooker Pot
  3. Add water with the same amount as rice. So if you have 3 cups of rice, add 3 cups of water
  4. Transfer Pot with rice and water in the Rice cooker
  5. When Rice cooker indicates "Cooked", Set and Serve!!!
